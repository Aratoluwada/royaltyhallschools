About RHS

Royalty Hall Schools

Building wholesome children through sound academic education, godly spiritual edification, and excellent moral upbringing.

OUR HISTORY

A school built with purpose.

2023 Where our journey began

Fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, Royalty Hall Schools commenced full operations in September 2023.

From the beginning, our goal has been clear: to provide children with an education that develops not only their intellect, but also their character, values and sense of purpose.

Today, that commitment continues through an academically sound and Christ-centred educational approach designed to raise a new generation of responsible leaders.

OUR PHILOSOPHY
“Total education for all children from all walks of life.”

We believe every child deserves an education that develops the whole person — intellectually, morally, socially and spiritually.

OUR MISSION

Building a wholesome child.

Building a wholesome child through sound academic education, godly spiritual edification, and excellent moral upbringing, training them up to be credible leaders of the nations.

OUR VISION

Raising responsible leaders.

To raise a new generation of responsible leaders by combining sound intellect, positive attitudes and godly values through quality education.

WHAT GUIDES US

OUR ACADEMIC APPROACH

Learning designed around the whole child.

Our curriculum is carefully structured to develop sound intellect, positive attitudes and godly values while giving children the foundation they need to become responsible leaders.

Early Years

A blend of the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) and Montessori system.

Upper Primary

A blend of the British curriculum and the Nigerian curriculum.

THE RHS EXPERIENCE

An environment built for growth.

Professional Teachers

Our classrooms are managed by professionally trained teachers committed to helping every child learn.

Collaborative Learning

Classrooms are arranged to encourage meaningful interaction between teachers and pupils.

Child Safety

Children are supervised throughout their stay in school, including during break and lunchtime.
