Our Management

Leadership with purpose.

Meet the people providing leadership and direction at Royalty Hall Schools, and discover the beliefs that shape our approach to raising wholesome children.

The people behind RHS

Guiding the journey from learning to leadership.

At Royalty Hall Schools, we believe that meaningful education requires purposeful leadership. Our management is committed to creating an environment where children are encouraged to grow academically, spiritually, morally and socially.

Director

Peace Toluwade

As the Director of Royalty Hall Schools, Rev Peace Toluwade contributes to the leadership and direction of the school, helping to foster an environment where academic development, godly values and excellent character go hand in hand.

The school's Christ-centred approach to education is reflected in his emphasis on spiritual edification, moral upbringing, discipline, respect and peaceful coexistence.

Proprietor

Joy Toluwade

As the Proprietor of Royalty Hall Schools, Joy Toluwade provides the vision and leadership that guide the school's commitment to quality education and the wholesome development of every child.

Her leadership reflects the school's belief that education should extend beyond academic achievement, helping children develop positive attitudes, sound values and the character needed to become responsible members of society.

Our Foundation

What We Believe

These principles influence the way we teach, nurture and interact with every child within the Royalty Hall Schools community.

01

Every Child Can Thrive

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to discover their abilities, develop confidence and receive quality education regardless of background.

02

Education Builds the Whole Person

Academic excellence matters, but so do character, discipline, positive attitudes, spiritual growth and practical life skills.

03

Faith Shapes Character

We believe Christian values should guide children toward integrity, compassion, responsibility and respect for others.

04

Excellence Is a Standard

We encourage children to approach learning and everyday life with diligence, curiosity and a commitment to doing their best.

05

Parents and School Are Partners

We believe meaningful education is strengthened when parents and teachers work together in the best interest of the child.

06

Every Child Deserves Respect

We promote an environment where children are treated with dignity, encouraged to coexist peacefully and protected from bullying.
